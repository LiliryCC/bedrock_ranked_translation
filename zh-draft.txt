HOW TO TRANSLATE
Make a copy of this file franslated,, eitehr replacing or putting translation 
on new line or whatever.

WHAT TO KEEP EXACTLY AS IT IS
- Words in {curly brackets}: see the list below. Do not translate them.
  You may move them to wherever your grammar needs them.
- \n means "new line". Keep it, and keep it in the same place if you can.
- Chat commands starting with /, for example /accept and /deny.
- Web links and file or folder names.
- Version numbers such as 1.16, 1.18+, 1.21+ and 26.50.
- Names of people.
- Names of products: Bedrock Ranked, Minecraft, Discord, Ko-fi, Xbox,
  Google Play, Windows, Android.
- Spaces at the start or end of a line do not matter, but keep double or
  triple spaces inside a line where a note asks for them.

TERMS PLAYERS USUALLY KEEP IN ENGLISH
Elo (skill rating), RTA and IGT, FOV, DNF. Keep them
unless your community has its own usual word. Terms from Minecraft (Nether,
Bastion, Stronghold, Ender Dragon, and so on) should match the official
Minecraft translation for your language.

THE TAGS IN THE NOTES
[WORD ORDER]  The game builds this line from parts. Write it as one natural
              sentence, moving the {curly brackets} wherever they belong.
[PLURAL]      This line has a number. English has two forms, one and many.
              If your language has more forms, add a # note and we will
              work out how to fit them.

WHAT THE {CURLY BRACKET} WORDS MEAN
{count} {number} {max} {yes} {needed} {votes} {players} {games} {wins} {losses}   a number
{seconds} a number of seconds     {percent} a number, without the % sign
{time} a time such as 12:34.5     {speed} a replay speed such as 2 or 0.5
{elo} {peak} a rating number     {change} a rating change such as +22
{fps} {low} {worst} performance numbers
{size} a file size such as 1.20 GB
{player} {name} {host} {world} {trophy} a player, world or trophy name
{mode} a game mode name            {rank} a rank such as Melon II
{season} a season number           {seed} {search} {token} {code} a value
{version} a version number         {path} {file} {server} a location
{error} {result} {status} {request} {field} {kind} text from the game
{setting} {value} {preference} {rule} {topic} {link} {gold} {beds}
   explained in the note on that line, or a word from the same section

1415 | Sprinkz Strat explanation
集合点策略讲解
1416 | Sprinkz strat is a strategy that allows you to always dig down into the starter staircase of the stronghold, based on the village bells. The reason why it works is that when stronghold generate under villages in bedrock, they generate from the world chunk of the village on the 4-4 relative coordinates of the chunk. Therefore, if we are able to map the starting coordinates of the village, we can just add 4-4 to those and we'll be able to know where to dig. It's therefore obvious that for each village center type there will be a different dig down spot. You will be guided for every single center type, there are 16 total. Since the starter staircase is decently wide, you don't need to dig at the exact spot, but it's enough to dig in the 5x5 area around it.
集合点策略是一种根据村庄的钟来定位、每次都能直接挖到要塞起始阶梯的策略。它之所以有效，是因为在基岩版中，当要塞生成在村庄下方时，它会从村庄所在区块内相对坐标 4-4 的位置开始生成。因此，只要能确定村庄的集合点（也就是村庄的初始坐标），再加上 4-4，就能知道该往哪里挖。显然，每种村庄中心类型都对应着不同的下挖位置。教程会带您逐一学习每种中心类型，总共有 16 种。由于起始阶梯相当宽，您不需要挖在精确的位置上，只要挖在它周围 5x5 的范围内就够了。
